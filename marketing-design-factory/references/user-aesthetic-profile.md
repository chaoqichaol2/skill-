# User Aesthetic Profile

Use this profile with `aesthetic-quality-bar.md`. Confirmed rules are mandatory. Provisional patterns describe the current delivery baseline and must be refined as the user adds cases.

## Confirmed Permanent Rules

- Classification tags: `中文 / 互联网 / 金融类`.
- Fit Chinese internet-finance operation design rather than game, casino, generic ecommerce, overseas fintech template, or traditional printed-bank advertising.
- Full-layout generation is allowed by default, including Chinese copy, CTA, and typography atmosphere. Verify the exact text; use controlled composition only when correction or editable delivery is needed.
- Place a small editable `广告` label in the upper-right safe area of every marketing image.
- Do not force staged approval gates. Pause only when the user requests a staged process or when a missing choice changes a business claim or delivery size.
- Reject any design with an obvious AI-generated appearance.
- Reject strange, dirty, unsupported, or incoherent colors.

## Current Positive Baseline

Evidence: user-provided final delivery files for lottery-result popups, fixed-deposit campaigns, large-denomination-certificate campaigns, and nine explicitly confirmed positive exports added on 2026-07-17: sports ticket, returning-customer reward, new-customer welcome, quick payment, digital-finance recruitment, wealth gift, local lifestyle, and shopping festival. These patterns are a proven delivery baseline, not an aesthetic ceiling. The two wealth-gift exports belong to one campaign family and must be combined when weighting repeated preferences.

The nine-image set is biased toward landscape banners: seven are 1035×300. Use it for aesthetic calibration, not as proof of popup, H5, component-library, brand-asset, safe-area, or editable-template rules. It does not complete the unfinished Immediate Design pages `01_品牌资产`, `02_营销组件库`, or `03_KV主视觉模板`.

### Composition

- Prefer one dominant hero-object group.
- For landscape banners, prefer a left-copy/right-hero reading path with clear separation rather than a centered vertical stack.
- For popup materials, keep a clear top headline, middle hero, and bottom CTA, but preserve meaningful whitespace between layers.
- Keep CTA subordinate to the headline and hero; it should not become the largest visual object.
- Reserve safe areas early instead of squeezing copy around generated decoration.
- For 1035×300-style banners, the most repeated structure is approximately 50–58% copy/CTA on the left and 35–45% hero or scene on the right.
- Use arcs, ribbons, ground curves, or contextual scenery to connect both sides without destroying the left-right hierarchy.
- In the confirmed nine-image set, 8 of 9 use left copy/right hero. Treat the sports scene as a campaign-specific title-spanning exception, not a competing default.

### Information Hierarchy

- Chinese product name or result state is the first reading level.
- Product benefit or next-step explanation is the second level.
- CTA is direct and short.
- Dynamic prize or amount information is isolated in a controlled field rather than blended into decorative art.
- In the confirmed nine-image set, 8 of 9 show a CTA. The no-button asset is an internal recruitment exception and does not justify removing CTA from consumer activity entry materials.

### Color

- Warm orange, red-orange, pale gold, and white are proven for deposit and campaign-entry materials.
- White or pale neutral cards with red accent actions are proven for result popups.
- Use dark backgrounds only when supported by the approved reference and keep them away from casino-like black-and-gold styling.
- Warm red, orange, coral, peach, pale gold, and white are the dominant personal palette for financial conversion campaigns.
- Use clear sky blue for sports or local-lifestyle themes and keep it airy; do not convert it into electric neon or blue-purple cyber styling.
- Keep one dominant color family, one supporting family, and limited small accents. Brand-app icons may retain native colors but must remain subordinate.
- Across the confirmed nine-image set, every design uses red or orange as a core emphasis colour; seven use a warm background, while sports and local-lifestyle designs use theme-supported sky blue. The rule is `warm activation plus scene adaptation`, not `make every design red`.

### Typography

- Use clean, bold Chinese sans-serif typography with strong size contrast.
- Strong display treatment, italic speed, white outlines, warm drop shadows, and even metallic/3D festival titles may be used when the campaign theme or approved reference supports them. Never use them as an automatic `premium` shortcut, and never reduce Chinese readability.
- Keep body copy neutral, calm, and easy to scan at phone scale.

### Hero Materials

- Use 3D or illustrated financial objects as an isolated hero layer: shield, hourglass, deposit certificate, coin group, gift, or approved campaign metaphor.
- Keep material detail concentrated on the hero; do not make headline, CTA, background, and every decoration use the same glossy 3D treatment.
- Use one lighting system and one restrained supporting motif such as a curve, grid, chart line, or particle family.
- Proven hero groups include red packets, gift boxes, coupons, benefit cards, mobile-payment devices, finance cards, coins, IP mascots, megaphones, football scenes, local-consumption scenes, and digital-finance information cards.
- Prefer hero groups that directly explain the business. Reject abstract rings, floating tokens, or generic AI-tech icons when they do not clarify the activity.

### Advertising-label Evidence

- The permanent forward rule remains: every new marketing image receives a small editable `广告` label in the upper-right safe area.
- Eight of the nine 2026-07-17 positive exports visibly contain `广告`. The internal recruitment asset is a historical contextual exception; it must not weaken the forward rule.

## Contextual Style Clusters

- Sports/local event: sky blue, stadium or city scene, brand mascot, red action accents, energetic diagonal movement.
- Customer reward/new customer: coral-red or peach background, coupon/packet/gift-box hero, large amount, approachable benefit language.
- Payment campaign: warm orange-red, mobile-payment device or app-icon group, card/packet/coin hero, clear monetary benefit.
- Digital-finance recruitment: orange-to-pink campaign gradient, megaphone or information-card hero, strong Chinese recruitment headline, structured secondary copy.
- Local lifestyle/shopping festival: bright blue or warm orange scene, IP characters or retail environment, festive headline, stronger promotion treatment.

## Confirmed Negative Example

Evidence: the two generated lottery-opportunity drafts rejected by the user as unattractive.

- Do not use giant gold beveled headlines as a default style; allow them only for approved festival-promotion contexts with controlled color and clear Chinese readability.
- Do not use dark brown/black plus gold as a default `premium` solution.
- Do not make headline, hero, particles, energy ring, and CTA all glow simultaneously.
- Do not let a glossy gradient pill button overwhelm the headline or hero; glossy CTA treatment is allowed when it matches the campaign style.
- Do not vertically center and symmetrically stack every layer.
- Do not turn a bank operation popup into a game-reward or casino screen.
- Do not copy only the reference object while losing its proportion, pacing, and restraint.
- Do not use default AI-tech electric-blue backgrounds, blue-purple cyber glow, random HUD frames, floating AI icons, generic AI characters, or fake interface fragments.
- Do not use casino or slot-game composition: competing blue/purple/red/green/gold, oversized reward numbers, multiple play buttons, timer panels, game eggs/hammers, or plastic arcade frames.
- Do not use unsupported rainbow gradients, dirty mixed hues, accidental green/purple/cyan contamination, or equal-weight high-saturation colors.
- Do not accept malformed objects, inconsistent lighting, conflicting perspective, duplicated decoration, unreadable micro-details, or over-smoothed plastic rendering.

## Permanent Negative-Prompt Block

Include the following meaning in every image-generation prompt:

`No obvious AI-generated look; no malformed geometry; no inconsistent perspective or shadows; no fake UI fragments; no random floating icons; no generic AI character; no cyber-blue or blue-purple neon glow unless explicitly required; no casino, slot-game, arcade, or game-reward interface; no dirty or unsupported color mixing; no equal-weight rainbow palette; no excessive glossy plastic; no meaningless micro-detail; no garbled Chinese text; no misspelled CTA; no inaccurate amount; no fake final-delivery logo.`

## Calibration Status

- Marketing intensity: medium-high by default; high is acceptable for sports, festival, shopping, and recruitment campaigns when hierarchy remains clear.
- Banking tone: modern, youthful, approachable, and conversion-oriented without casino/game behavior.
- Decoration density: medium; richer scene density is acceptable when the copy zone remains clean.
- Preferred composition: provisional asymmetrical for banners; controlled vertical hierarchy for popups.

Update these provisional values only after explicit user feedback or additional approved cases.
