# 已交付金融运营 Banner 正向案例库 V1

## Evidence Status

- Classification: `中文 / 互联网 / 金融类`.
- User status: all nine images were explicitly confirmed as final delivered positive examples on 2026-07-17.
- Source type: flattened PNG exports; no editable layer, font, component, or source-template claims may be inferred.
- Reuse scope: global aesthetic calibration and reference routing. Do not treat this set as a complete popup, H5, component-library, or brand-asset specification.
- Weighting: `wealth-gift-hot-activity` and `wealth-gift-banner` are one campaign family and count as one independent aesthetic signal.

## Cross-Case Evidence

- 8 of 9 designs use a left-copy/right-hero hierarchy; the sports case is a title-spanning scene exception.
- 8 of 9 designs contain an explicit CTA; the internal recruitment case is the only valid no-button exception.
- 8 of 9 designs visibly place `广告` in the upper-right; the internal recruitment case is not consumer advertising evidence.
- 9 of 9 use red or orange as a core emphasis colour. Seven use a warm background; sports and local-lifestyle cases use scene-supported sky blue.
- The repeated reading path is campaign title → amount or core benefit → CTA.
- Hero groups are concrete business metaphors—ticket/stadium, benefit card, gift box, payment device, wallet, coupon, food, shopping street, or recruitment megaphone—not generic abstract AI decoration.
- Consumer promotions may use high marketing intensity; internal recruitment and restrained finance entries must reduce festive effects and visual noise.

## Production Use

1. Select at most two independent examples that match the current business, role, and intensity.
2. Extract measurable composition, palette, material, lighting, and density traits; never copy embedded claims or licensed assets.
3. Prefer the corpus as aesthetic evidence. Use source templates and slot specifications for exact layout, components, and export rules.
4. Record exceptions explicitly. A contextual exception must not overwrite the global rule.
